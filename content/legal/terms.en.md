2. **Own legal pages.** The customer provides its own imprint and privacy policy for its academy and keeps them up to date; without both, no course can be published in the service. It informs its learners about the processing of their data, including that an AI assesses their work first.
3. **No promise of formal qualifications.** The Certificate of Completion ("Abschlussbescheinigung") confirms that a learner has completed a course and states how, for example with reviewed work or a passed final test. It is not a formal or state qualification. The customer does not present it as one and uses no terms that promise a formal, official or state qualification. The service points out such terms and does not allow them in titles and certificates.
4. **Distance learning.** The customer offers no distance learning through the service that requires approval under the German Distance Learning Protection Act (FernUSG), as long as it does not hold that approval.
5. **Lawful use.** The customer does not use the service for illegal content, unsolicited advertising, malware or to deceive learners. It does not attempt to access other academies' data, circumvent technical limits or safeguards, or put undue load on the service, for example through automated mass requests.
6. **Advertising to learners.** The customer sends newsletters and other advertising only to learners who consented in the service and confirmed their consent by e-mail, and from its own systems.
7. **Reachability.** The customer keeps a contact address up to date through which we can reach it, and answers enquiries about reports concerning its academy's content without undue delay.
