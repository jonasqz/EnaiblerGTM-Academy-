# Idea brief: automatic invoice follow-ups for freelance designers

**Customer.** Freelance designers in Germany with 5–15 active clients who invoice monthly.

**Problem.** They lose around four hours a month chasing late invoices and delay
payments to themselves; two interviewees had to dip into savings last quarter.

**What I learned (7 interviews).**

- "I hate writing the second reminder. It feels like begging." (Interview 2)
- Five of seven track invoices in a spreadsheet and forget follow-ups.
- Three already pay for accounting software but do not use its reminder feature
  because the tone is "too corporate".

**Riskiest assumption.** Designers will let a tool send reminders in their name.
**Next test.** A concierge test with 10 designers for four weeks: I send the
reminders by hand in their tone. Success: at least 6 of 10 want to continue and
agree to pay €9 per month.
